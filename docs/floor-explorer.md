# Floor exploration

This is a preliminary evidence review, not a legal or code determination. Final decisions are made by LADBS and licensed professionals.

The interaction follows [FireSight](https://github.com/Devaj6190/FireSight): separate individual floors, focus a numbered floor, and isolate that floor while preserving selection. References inspected at commit `61f9677567492387330dac760638ab5137765cf5`: `web/src/explode.ts`, `web/src/store.ts`, `web/src/Rooms.tsx`, and `web/src/cutawayGeometry.ts`. SecondStay implements this interaction in its own Three.js scene. FireSight's generated interior layouts are not building evidence and are not imported.

Interior geometry is loaded from private Matterport caches. A single-floor scan is associated with a building floor by its listing or explicit caption. Local scan labels such as Floor 1 are not substituted for the building floor. Multi-floor scan assets without an explicit mapping and floors inferred only from suite digits are withheld. DXF LINE entities are linework, not classified walls; their vertical extrusion is illustrative. Neither the position nor orientation of a scan inside the building is surveyed here. Each floor slab repeats the building footprint, with evenly estimated story heights.

For a source available only as an image, choose a local PNG, JPEG, or WebP in the floor panel. The browser extracts horizontal and vertical line candidates within a user-selected crop. Review the overlay before using the draft. This is limited local image processing, not a vision-model reconstruction: text, furniture and dimensions can still produce false candidates; diagonal walls, curves, door symbols and room semantics are not reconstructed. Results are **read from images, not measured**. Scale, position, orientation and extrusion height are illustrative. Drafts stay in browser memory and are cleared when leaving the scene. CoStar images are not downloaded by the server or read through a CORS workaround.

Recorded Matterport opening widths, heights, sill elevations and positions cut holes into the wall geometry. Window glazing and doorway frames make these openings visible; colors, frame thickness and materials are illustrative. A doorway is not identified as a building entrance. Missing vertical dimensions use display assumptions. Closed, non-void room boundaries receive separate floor colors without inventing room functions. Shared wall openings are counted once. Separating floors keeps the stack aligned and offsets only the selected floor for inspection; that offset is an interaction, not the suite's surveyed position.

Checks: `python -m pytest`; `node --test tests/test_floor_motion.mjs tests/test_intro_framing.cjs tests/test_scan_viewer.cjs`.

The floor directory indexes all listing plans and scans with a confirmed floor association, plus building plans with an explicit floor caption or an unambiguous suite listing. Each floor lists every linked source, with separate options for multiple suites or scans. Selecting a floor opens its available interior geometry; image-only sources open as original 2D plans in the viewer. Original scan tours remain accessible when local vectors are unavailable. Sources without a confirmed floor appear separately. Viewer source selection is retained in the URL independently of the checklist's review scope; a review link changes the checklist to the viewed suite. Images are displayed directly in the browser and are not downloaded or converted into invented 3D interiors.

Private reconstructed interiors can be supplied in `data/raw/interior_models/<property>.json` (version 1). Models must reference an existing listing on the same floor and name their source and scale basis. Measured scans retain metre dimensions. Three non-collinear source/target control points establish a rigid rotation and translation; no resizing to rentable area is performed. A discrepancy above 0.5 m withholds registration. The numerical fit residual describes agreement between picked controls, not survey accuracy. Room holes and openings are transformed with the walls.

Registered models on a floor are assembled in a shared plan frame and shown against that source's floor perimeter. Geographic north requires separate site controls; the plan viewer does not overlay these frames on aerial imagery. A full-floor listing can use its own sufficiently extensive closed scan boundary as an intrinsic floor frame. Models without location controls are displayed separately from the building slab. All source images remain reachable; a linked image does not imply that it has already been reconstructed. Printed room dimensions can calibrate a traced image; those observations remain **read from images, not measured**. Public broker documents may describe a different fit-out or contain links copied from another property, and must be checked before use.

Closed scan boundaries remain usable when the export also contains dangling lines or a connector between a perimeter and a shaft. Boundary extraction removes graph bridges and retains closed, unambiguous loops, without drawing new edges across gaps. Intersecting cycles with unresolved branches remain unfilled. Original wall segments and opening positions are retained.

The plan gallery exposes every attached plan with lazy image loading. Attachment location (building record or listing) does not establish whole-floor coverage. The floor source selector distinguishes available 3D geometry from a linked 2D image and indicates which models have a floor frame. Private model loading validates openings, nested room boundaries and source links; placement is recomputed from control points rather than accepted from a stored registration field.

Sheets without printed lengths can supply explicitly unitless reconstructions (`geometry.units: "plan_relative"`). These preserve drawing proportions and use illustrative extrusion heights; they are labelled as drafts with no physical scale. A matching relative floor frame may place a suite within its own key plan, but it cannot receive a measured scan or a model in metres. Relative drawings are isolated from the physical building stack. Registration residuals for those drawings are expressed in plan units, never metres. A whole-floor sheet can define its own drawing frame without establishing geographic north or measured size.

The private example reconstructions were manually transcribed from browser-displayed plans and public broker sheets. They are not automated vision-agent outputs, their accuracy has not been independently checked, and ambiguous small symbols are omitted. Plan reconstruction drafts are labelled separately from scan geometry. Linked originals remain available for comparison. These building-specific models and research notes remain in ignored `data/raw/`, so a fresh Git checkout does not include them.

A partial floor assembly uses a dashed reference perimeter instead of a filled floor surface. Its coverage panel names the displayed parts, links other suite models that are not placed in the shared frame, and lists spaces without linked geometry. Unrepresented interior areas carry no occupancy inference. A whole-floor source retains its floor surface; neither case resizes a suite to fill the available outline.

## What is on record for each floor

Every floor carries a breakdown (`static/floor_accounting.mjs`) of its area and why each part is or is not drawn: rooms drawn from scan walls or a traced plan; suites on the market whose 3D model exists but is not yet placed on the floor; suites with only a CoStar plan image; suites with only a 3D tour without wall data; suites with nothing on record; area leased to current tenants (CoStar tenancies by floor; tenant plans are not public); and the remainder with no record at all. Floor size comes from CoStar's floor record, else the Vexcel footprint. A suite on the market can still be occupied, so listed and leased areas can overlap; the remainder is never negative.

Areas with no layout on record are drawn as a 10 ft reference grid clipped to the floor outline, never as invented rooms. On a floor with no linked source the grid covers the whole footprint; on a floor drawn in a registered frame it sits under the drawn rooms. A suite model whose position on the floor is not linked has no frame to grid.
